PandaX prepares the file. You assess it.

Manufacturers arrive at an assessment with evidence scattered across scanners, spreadsheets and email. PandaX collects it into one reviewable package. That shortens your intake, and it does not touch your judgement.

Four guardrails.

Guardrails
Scanner-neutralPandaX ingests output from any scanner and does not replace tooling you or your client already use. If a client brings their own analysis, it becomes an input, not a competitor.
No conformity opinionsPandaX never issues certificates, verdicts or conformity opinions, and the product is built so it cannot imply one. Findings are recorded as observations with their sources; the assessment is yours.
Portable exportThe evidence package is an open format the manufacturer owns, readable without PandaX installed. Nothing locks a client to us or routes them to a particular reviewer.
Pricing independent of outcomePricing is per workspace and never tied to the outcome of an assessment. There is no arrangement under which PandaX earns more from a favourable result.
