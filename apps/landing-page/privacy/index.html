Privacy

Last updated 13 September 2026.

This notice explains what happens to personal data when you use this website. It is short because the site does very little with it.

Who is responsible

PandaX operates this website and decides how the data described here is handled. Questions about this notice, or any request below, can go to contact@pandax.online.

What the site collects

This website sets no non-essential cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party content. It collects contact details only when you choose to submit the contact form.

Server logs

To deliver and secure the site, the hosting provider records standard request data: IP address, time, requested file, referrer and user agent. These logs are kept only as long as that purpose requires. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

If you write to us

The details you send through the contact form or by email are used to answer you, and kept for as long as the enquiry requires. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. The form posts to an endpoint on this domain. Cloudflare processes the request as our hosting provider, and Resend delivers the message to our email inbox.

Fonts and images

Fonts and images are served from this domain. The page makes no requests to third-party content or analytics providers.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. and served from its global edge network, which means the server nearest you answers the request. Cloudflare handles this data as our hosting provider.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. You can exercise any of these by writing to contact@pandax.online. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection supervisory authority.
