What the Act asks you to keep

Not a scan report. A file that shows what you shipped, what was wrong with it, and what you did about it. Kept for ten years.

  1. 11 Sep 2026 Vulnerability-handling requirements apply In effect
  2. 11 Dec 2027 Full CRA application
  • Cybersecurity risk assessments, software architecture designs, test reports. Companies must keep these available to authorities for at least 10 years.
  • Penalties can reach up to €15 million or 2.5% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.

European Commission Digital Strategy; Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Articles 71 and 73.

The clock starts when you know

Both deadlines run from the moment you become aware, not from the moment you have a fix.

  1. 24 hours

    Early warning

    Alerting for an actively exploited vulnerability or severe incident.

  2. 72 hours

    Notification

    Affected product information, measures taken and user protection steps.

  3. 14 days

    Actively exploited vulnerability

    Final report, once a corrective or mitigating measure becomes available.

  4. 1 month

    Severe incident

    Final report, after the 72-hour notification.

Reports are submitted through the CRA Single Reporting Platform.

Who we are

An independent European workspace for product security evidence.

The record is the product.

Scanners find things. Managed services do the work on your behalf. Neither of them owns the document at the end. The technical file falls out of somebody else's tool, and gets assembled by hand again on the next release. That part is ours.

What we hold to

  • Never paid on the outcome
