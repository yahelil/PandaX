PandaX prepares the record. You assess it.

Manufacturers arrive at an assessment with evidence scattered across tools, spreadsheets and email. PandaX collects it into one reviewable record. That shortens your intake, and it does not touch your judgement.

Four guardrails

Guardrails
Scanner-neutralWe ingest output from any scanner and replace nothing you or your client already use. A client's own analysis becomes an input, not a competitor.
No conformity opinionsNo certificates, no verdicts, no conformity opinions — and the product is built so it cannot imply one. Findings are recorded as observations with their sources. The judgement stays with the assessor.
Portable exportThe record is an open format the manufacturer owns, readable without PandaX. Nothing locks a client to us or routes them to a particular reviewer.
Outcome-independent pricingPricing is per workspace and never tied to the result of an assessment. There is no arrangement under which PandaX earns more from a favourable outcome.
