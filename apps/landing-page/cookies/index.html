Cookies

Last updated 13 September 2026.

This website sets no cookies. That is the whole notice, but the detail is below, because a page that simply asserts it is worth no more than one that does not.

Nothing is stored on your device

No cookies, no local storage, no session storage, and no other identifier. Nothing is written when you arrive, and nothing is read when you come back. Opening the site twice is indistinguishable from opening it once.

So there is no consent banner

The consent requirement in Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, and in § 25 TDDDG in Germany, applies to storing or reading information on your device. Because nothing is stored or read, the requirement does not arise. A banner here would be asking permission for something that does not happen.

No analytics, and no third parties

There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no embedded video, no chat widget and no third-party form embed. The fonts are served from this domain rather than from Google Fonts. Loading this page makes no request to any host but ours.

What the contact form does

The form posts what you type to an endpoint on this domain, which forwards it to us so that we can reply. It sets no cookie and leaves nothing behind on your device. What happens to the message afterwards is described in the privacy notice.

If this changes

Adding anything that stores data on your device would change what this page has to say, and would bring a consent requirement with it. This notice would be updated first, not afterwards.
