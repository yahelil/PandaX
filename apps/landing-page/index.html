The CRA makes product security a legal obligation

New EU rules apply to software and connected products.

  • Companies must secure products and manage vulnerabilities throughout the product lifecycle.
  • Incident reporting starts 11 September 2026. Full application follows on 11 December 2027.
  • Penalties can reach €15 million or 2.5% of worldwide annual revenue.

In effect 11 September 2026

Every release doesn't just need a security checklist. It needs evidence.

European Commission Digital Strategy; Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Articles 71 and 73.

When proof is requested, teams reconstruct compliance by hand

Product security and compliance tools leave evidence fragmented.

  • Product security tools find risks, but do not assemble CRA proof.
  • General compliance tools track policy, but not each product release.
  • Jira, CI/CD, SBOM tools, documents and legal workflows hold the evidence, and stay disconnected.

Questions worth asking

Do you need our source code?

No. PandaX works from the SBOM. Your source code never leaves your environment and PandaX never receives or views it.

What if we don't have an SBOM?

PandaX provides the tools to generate one, without sharing source code.

Does this make us CRA-compliant?

No. PandaX produces the documentation and records the process. Conformity is assessed by a body, not by a tool.

Who decides what gets submitted to a regulator?

The company. Nothing is submitted without explicit authorization, and every submission keeps a time-stamped audit trail.

How is this different from a vulnerability scanner?

A scanner finds risks. It does not assemble the proof. PandaX connects the release, the findings, the decisions and the documents into one record a reviewer can read.

What happens to our records if we stop being a customer?

The export is an open format and it is yours. It is built to be read without PandaX, which is also what makes it useful to a reviewer.

What exists today, and what is still being built?

Today: a working proof of concept that takes a release, builds the inventory and findings, records decisions, generates the documents and seals the result. Being built: the hosted workspace, connectors and scheduled monitoring.
