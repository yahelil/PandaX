Actis

The product. A release goes in, a reviewable record comes out.

Actis is built by PandaX. It reads an SBOM, matches it against public advisory sources, records what you decide about each finding, generates the documents, and seals the result to the release that shipped.

What it covers

  1. 01

    Release evidence mapping

    Links each product release to SBOMs, tests, risks, tickets and approvals.

  2. 02

    Gap review

    Checks what you have against what Annex VII asks for, and names what is missing.

  3. 03

    Carries forward

    Keeps the record current when the product changes, and when the rules do.

  4. 04

    Evidence history

    Maintains dated, protected evidence for the retention period the CRA requires.

  5. 05

    Drafts the documents

    The Annex VII technical file and the Annex V declaration, in the form they are asked for.

  6. 06

    Files the reports

    Takes reports you have approved through the single reporting platform, and keeps the receipt.

What exists today

Working today

  • Ingest a release and build the component inventory.
  • Match against public advisory sources, with failed sources shown as failed.
  • Record decisions with their rationale, author and date.
  • Generate the documents and seal the result.
  • Verify a sealed bundle from a clean machine, with a key supplied separately.

Being built

  • The hosted workspace, for more than one person.
  • Connectors into Jira, CI/CD and document systems.
  • Scheduled advisory monitoring that opens cases on its own.
